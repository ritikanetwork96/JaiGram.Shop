JaiGram Legal & Privacy Center

Transparent policies, verified security & customer rights
Strict Data Minimization Guarantee We collect ONLY your Full Name & Email ID (and transaction UTR entered by you). We NEVER ask for or collect banking passwords, card PINs, CVVs, contacts, or private files.

1. What Information We Collect (Name & Email Only)

At JaiGram Shop, privacy is built into our core. We only request the absolute bare minimum details necessary to deliver digital purchases to you:

  • Customer Name: Used exclusively to personalize your customer vault greeting and invoices.
  • Email Address: Used for secure single-use Email OTP login verification, order confirmation receipts, and permanent digital access link delivery.
  • Payment Reference / Transaction UTR: When you complete a UPI or Binance Pay transaction, you enter the reference ID so our automated system can match the payment with your order.

2. What We Strictly NEVER Collect

  • ❌ NO Debit/Credit Card Details: We never collect, see, or store 16-digit card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, or ATM PINs.
  • ❌ NO Banking Passwords: We never ask for your NetBanking credentials, UPI MPIN, or bank login info.
  • ❌ NO Phone Tracking: We do not require or store your mobile phone number, phonebook contacts, or SMS messages.
  • ❌ NO Data Sharing: Your email and name are strictly private. We never sell, rent, or distribute user details to third parties, ad networks, or brokers.

3. Discreet Digital Vault Delivery

All purchased digital content, streaming links, and cloud storage folders are delivered directly inside your encrypted Orders Vault. Your order history is completely private to your account.

256-Bit SSL Encrypted Payment Rails Payments are routed directly through official UPI apps or Binance Pay. Zero banking credentials touch or store on our servers.

1. How Payments Work on JaiGram

We use direct peer-to-peer and bank-to-bank settlement methods:

  • Instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM): You scan our official dynamic merchant QR code or copy our official UPI ID. You authorize the payment securely inside your own trusted banking app. JaiGram never sees your bank account login or MPIN.
  • Binance Pay & Crypto: Fast, decentralized digital asset settlement with zero card fraud risk and instant blockchain / Pay ID verification.

2. Zero Financial Credential Storage

Because payments are initiated by you within your preferred banking app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Binance app, JaiGram does not possess or store any financial credentials. Your money moves directly and safely through NPCI-authorized banking channels.

3. 256-Bit HTTPS Session Security

Every communication between your web browser and JaiGram is guarded by SHA-256 TLS 1.3 encryption, ensuring your session is completely protected against eavesdropping and tampering.

Digital Creator Marketplace Terms Simple, common-sense rules governing digital access passes and store purchases.

1. User Eligibility

You must be of the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction to browse and complete purchases on JaiGram Shop.

2. Personal Access Rights

  • All digital vault passes and media links are provided for your own personal, private collection viewing.
  • Public redistribution, unauthorized mass reselling, or dumping into public forums is strictly prohibited.

3. Instant Digital Delivery

Once your payment is verified, digital access passes are activated and stored inside your Orders Vault. Delivery takes only seconds under normal operation.

Lifetime Link Replacement Guarantee Never lose access to your purchases. Free replacement links anytime via 24/7 VIP Support.

1. Free Lifetime Replacement

If any cloud storage link, Google Drive pass, or Mega folder ever goes down or quota exceeds, simply message our support with your order reference. A fresh, working replacement link is issued promptly at zero extra cost.

2. 100% Refund Scenarios

  • Duplicate Payment: If you were charged twice due to a UPI or banking delay, the duplicate charge is refunded immediately.
  • Non-Delivery: If we fail to deliver your digital access within 12 hours of verified payment, you receive an immediate 100% refund.
  • Permanent Unavailability: If a collection is permanently unavailable, you may choose an alternative pack of equal value or receive a full 100% refund.
